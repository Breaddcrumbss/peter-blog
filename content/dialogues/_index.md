The dialogues in this section explore questions and issues prompted by particular political events, by authors or by biblical books. They are offered as reflective engagements rather than professional or academic critiques. I have written them with educated readers in mind who have an interest in the subjects under discussion.

They are intended to invite thought rather than to settle questions.
